## Important Definitions

- **Photosynthesis** — The process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.
- **Thylakoid membranes** — Internal chloroplast membranes where the light-dependent reactions occur.
- **Calvin cycle** — The light-independent stage in the stroma that fixes carbon dioxide into glucose using ATP and NADPH.
- **Stroma** — The fluid surrounding the thylakoids inside a chloroplast; site of the Calvin cycle.
- **RuBisCO** — The enzyme catalyzing the first major step of carbon fixation; likely the most abundant protein on Earth.
